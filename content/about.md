I am a JPL Postdoctoral fellow working under the supervision of [Marc Simard](https://swot.jpl.nasa.gov/people.htm?id=52) using airborne and satellite SAR imagery to estimate biomass and detect forest disturbances. I apply tools from computer vision and variational image processing to analyze backscatter and coherence images.
